Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is Organiser free?

Organiser is currently free to use while it's in beta. We haven't announced pricing beyond that yet.

What devices does it work on?

Organiser is built natively for iPhone and iPad, using Apple's own frameworks.

Is my data private?

Yes. Organiser takes a local-first approach — your dates, commitments and financial information are stored on your device rather than a remote server. Read the full Privacy Policy for details.

Does Organiser replace my banking app?

No — Budget isn't trying to be your banking app. It helps you understand the regular spending you've already committed to, based on the bills, subscriptions and income you tell it about.

When can I download it?

Organiser is currently in UK beta. We're not able to share an exact public launch date yet.

Does Organiser work outside the UK?

Right now Organiser is built around UK life — £ currency, UK bank holidays and UK-specific categories like MOT and vehicle tax. Support for other regions isn't available yet.
